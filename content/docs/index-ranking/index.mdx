# Stunt Double Index

More of your visitors arrive through an AI agent: someone asks ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini or Perplexity to compare products, find an answer or check out on their
behalf. The [Stunt Double Index](https://index.stuntdouble.io) measures how that
goes on your site, and ranks it against everyone else.

Each tracked domain gets a score out of 100, a band and a rank, overall and
within its sector. Index data is public, so you can read any tracked site's
report, including your competitors'.

## How a site is scored

A score comes from two kinds of evidence:

- **Probes.** Quick HTTP checks that look at what agents depend on, such as
  whether the site is reachable, says what agents may do, and publishes the
  files and structured data agents look for. Each failing check is listed with
  its weight.
- **Agent sessions.** Live sessions in a real browser, where each AI provider
  tries the jobs a customer would: research the brand, its prices and
  policies; go from the homepage to signing up or checking out; find how an
  agent could act for a customer; and find how to reach the business. A
  separate grader marks each session against a fixed rubric, and each session
  records what the agent tried, its evidence, a summary and the frictions it
  hit. Each model is also asked, with no browser, how the site compares with
  its competitors.

What the agents managed counts for more than the probes. The combined score is
then calibrated against every other site the Index scores, so a number reads
the same way in every sector.

A full run is the probes plus about 24 agent sessions. The top 50 sites get a
monthly run of the main agent sessions; other sites get the probes. Stunt Double
runs the sessions on the same [browser toolset](/stuntkit/browser-toolset) its
actors use.

## What it measures

| Category                | Can an agent...                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brand awareness         | Recognize the brand and what it offers                  |
| Discovery               | Find and recommend the site for a person's request      |
| Information retrieval   | Read the facts it needs from the site                   |
| Market ranking          | Place the site among its competitors                    |
| Accuracy                | Report what the site says without getting it wrong      |
| Task completion         | Carry out a task end to end, on a person's behalf       |
| Delegated access        | Act for a signed-in person, safely                      |
| Contact & communication | Find a way to reach the business                        |

Each category has a weight, and Task completion carries the most. Delegated
access is scored only for sites with a sign-in: a site with nothing to sign in
to has nothing to delegate.

The report also breaks the score down by provider, so you can see when one
agent struggles where the others don't.

<Callout variant="note" title="No score?">
  A site can be tracked without a score when the checks were blocked or
  unanswered, or the site opted out. The report says which.
</Callout>

## Add or claim a site

Anyone signed in can add a site that isn't tracked yet at
[index.stuntdouble.io](https://index.stuntdouble.io). A new site gets light
probes by default, with an option to add live agent sessions, and becomes
public on the leaderboard. Sites that opt out in `robots.txt` aren't scored.

Claim your domain with an email address on that domain: `sam@acme.com` can
claim `acme.com` and its subdomains. Claiming lets you re-score the site after
you ship changes (see [Improve your score](/index-ranking/improve-your-score)),
and adds it as a project in your Stunt Double workspace, if you have one.

## Share and look up a score

Each scorecard has:

- **A badge** to embed, in a full, compact or 20px README style, as HTML or
  Markdown.
- **A fix prompt** to paste into your coding agent, built from the
  frictions agents hit on the site.
- **An MCP prompt**: one message that asks your AI assistant about the site
  and tells it how to connect to the Index's
  [MCP server](/mcp/connect#index-lookups-with-no-account), which needs no
  account.

The [reports](https://index.stuntdouble.io/reports) page lists the Index's
published reports: fixed, citable snapshots of how agents experience a set of
sites, with a PDF to download once one is ready.
