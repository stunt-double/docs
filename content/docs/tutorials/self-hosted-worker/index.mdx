# Test a product behind your firewall

If the product you want to test is only reachable inside your network, a
cloud browser can't reach it. A device is a Mac on your side of the firewall
running the Stunt Double app, so actors browse from there without you opening
anything up.

<Callout variant="note" title="Plans">
  Devices are on every paid plan, and testing private or internal addresses from
  one needs the Startup plan or above. There is nothing to switch on: any member
  can set up their own Mac.
</Callout>

## Set up a Mac

1. On a Mac that can reach your internal product, download the Stunt Double
   [desktop app](/integrations/desktop-app), open it and sign in. Google
   Chrome needs to be installed.
2. Open **Devices** from the sidebar, or **Go > This Device** (Cmd+D).
3. Under **This Mac**, click **Set up this Mac** and wait for it to show
   **Connected**.

The app dials out to Stunt Double rather than waiting for an inbound
connection, so your firewall needs no changes. If your product sits behind a
VPN portal or SSO, open the browser under **Live view** and sign in once.
The sign-in persists for later runs. If the sign-in needs a client certificate,
Keychain or a smart card, switch **Where the browser runs** to **Its own
window** first.

## Point a project at it

When you save a project's address, Stunt Double works out whether it is public
or only reachable inside your network, and the address field says which as
you type. Runs on an internal project go to a device automatically: an actor
on the cloud browser borrows whichever device is online, and if none is, the
run stops up front and tells you.

## Choose where an actor's browser runs

To keep an actor on a device for every run, open the actor and go to its
**Browser** tab:

- **Cloud browser** for public sites. Internal projects still use any online
  device.
- **Device**, then **Any device in this workspace** to use whichever Mac is
  online, or a named Mac to always use that one.

An actor set to a device never falls back to the cloud: if its device is
offline, the run stops and tells you. Runs that share a device take turns, one
browser at a time.

## Control what it can reach

Owners and admins can open a device from **Devices** and set **Network
access**:

- **No restriction**: actors can open any site.
- **Only these domains**: only the domains you list.
- **Any domain except these**: everything except the domains you list.

A new device starts with no restriction, so it works as soon as it connects.
To restrict it, switch to Only these domains and list only what your actors
need to reach. **Test a URL** on the same page checks the device can open an
address before a run depends on it.

## Watch status live

Devices show their status live everywhere they appear: the **Devices** list,
the actor's **Browser** tab and **This Mac** in the app. A Mac that stops
checking in turns Offline within a couple of minutes, so you can tell before
starting a run that depends on it.
