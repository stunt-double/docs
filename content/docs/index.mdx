# Stunt Double documentation

Stunt Double gives you authentic user insights from autonomous AI agents,
backed by evidence. **Actors**, AI users you describe, drive a real browser
through your product and come back with a recording, screenshots and the steps
behind every result.

A workspace holds your team, and a project holds one product. Inside it,
actors do three kinds of work, and every run comes back as evidence.
